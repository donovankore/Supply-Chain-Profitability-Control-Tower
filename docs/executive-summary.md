# Executive summary

## Business question

Where are supplier, inventory, and fulfillment failures reducing contribution margin, and what intervention should leadership prioritize?

## Finding

Across 35,958 validated order lines, the business produced $11.72M in net revenue and $4.01M in contribution margin. Aggregate margin remained healthy at 34.2%, but on-time delivery was 92.9% against a 95% target and stockouts reached 10.6% against an 8% target.

Using conservative recovery assumptions, the model estimates $109K in annualized opportunity across late-freight efficiency and stockout exposure.

## Recommendation

Launch a 60-day supplier recovery sprint:

1. Prioritize the bottom three suppliers by on-time performance and revenue exposure.
2. Recalculate reorder points for the highest-risk SKUs.
3. Establish a weekly OTIF exception review with supplier owners.
4. Track recovered freight cost, stockout rate, and contribution margin as outcome metrics.

## Measurement plan

Success should be defined as on-time delivery at or above 95%, stockout rate below 8%, and validated financial recovery against a Finance-approved baseline.

## Important limitation

The dataset is synthetic and the opportunity estimate is illustrative. A production deployment requires calibration with actual purchase orders, inventory snapshots, logistics invoices, contracts, and lost-sales estimates.
